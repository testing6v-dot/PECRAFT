# PESMP feature catalogue

## Daily play loop
- Daily login reward: fixed, configurable amount; 24-hour cooldown; no paid spins or random cash prizes.
- Daily/weekly missions: mine, farm, explore, fish, trade, build and playtime goals.
- Streak cosmetics: titles, chat styles and particles; streaks never remove earned currency.
- Player profile: playtime, balance, kills/deaths in opted-in PvP modes, missions completed and first-join date.
- Player shops and auction listings with fees and moderation logs.
- Homes, warps, TPA, messaging, ignore and social groups.
- Server guide with command search and Bedrock-friendly text fallbacks.

## Staff console
- Staff mode with protected inventory and clear exit restoration.
- Vanish and staff chat.
- Freeze with reason and automatic audit entry.
- Warn/mute/kick/tempban with reason, duration and actor recorded.
- Inventory inspection restricted to trusted staff and logged.
- Reports queue with claim/resolve status.
- Emergency maintenance mode blocks new joins without deleting player data.
- Owner dashboard reports TPS, MSPT, memory and online count where the host exposes them.

## Original events
- Build-of-the-week judged by staff with transparent criteria.
- Community scavenger hunt with fixed clue/reward sets.
- Parkour time trials and leaderboards.
- Seasonal cosmetic collections earned by missions.
- Tournament brackets for opt-in PvP arenas.

## Safety and fairness
- No real-money gambling, paid random rewards or pay-to-win hearts.
- No staff command grants OP to arbitrary players.
- Moderation reasons and audit logs are mandatory.
- Keep SMP, Lifesteal and PvP inventories/economies isolated.
- Protect lobby and spawn; PvP is opt-in and disabled in safe zones.
